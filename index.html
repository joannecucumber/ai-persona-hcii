HCII · AI Persona × Design Research

From Static Snapshot
to Living System

The Evolution of AI Personas and the Researcher's Reflective Practice

簡潔安 (Jie-An Chien)　·　Advisor: 唐玄輝 (Hsien-Hui Tang)
Department of Design, National Taiwan University of Science and Technology
Background

Personas are powerful, yet static

  • Personas anchor design in users, yet once written they freeze, missing how users shift with context and emotion
  • Generative AI turns a persona into an interactive agent you can talk to, not just read
  • But the differences between an AI persona and a real participant remain underexplored
Related Work & Research Gap

From "how human-like" to "how different"

  • Cooper：personas anchor design in user goals, but remain documents to read
  • Park et al.：concrete life-context makes AI roles believable directly inspired our six dimensions
  • Role-playing LLMs：capable, but prone to role drift and inconsistency
  • Existing AI-persona work follows a fidelity approach："how human-like?"

We take a difference-analysis approach. The difference itself is the productive signal.

Research Questions

Two questions

  • RQ1　How can we construct consistent and discriminable AI personas for design research?
  • RQ2　What are the structural differences in linguistic performance between AI personas and human participants?

Rather than asking whether AI personas resemble humans, we ask where they differ, and what that means for design research.

Method · Action Research

Two studies, one context

Approach

An action-research process of human-AI co-construction, then evaluation through two studies.

Context：Gen Z ETF investors

Strong emotional-behavioral coupling："I know I should hold long-term, but I can't help checking the market every hour."

Construct · Three-stage data collection (Gemini 2.5)

How the persona is built

① Secondary

Gemini as a market analyst, producing an exploratory report on Gen Z ETF investing.

② Primary

Gemini as an extraction assistant, pulling real first-person posts from PTT & Dcard (the authentic voice).

③ Script

Gemini as a scriptwriter, integrating it into a complete six-dimension character script.

④ Deploy

Script as system instructions in GPTs, creating an interviewable persona.

GPTs fix the instructions in the backend, so the role isn't overwritten across multi-turn conversations.

Six-Dimension Script Methodology

Six dimensions, three functions

Basic Informationidentity · consistency
Worldviewbelief · decision logic
Psychological Stateemotional profile
Behavioral Patternseveryday coping
Linguistic Repertoiretone · slang · markers
Daily Life Scenariosconcrete scenes

Suppress drift (Basic + Worldview) · enable agency (Behavior + Scenarios) · enhance reflection (Psych + Linguistic)

Construction Output · Two contrasting personas

Two ends of one axis：source of confidence

Xiao-An

Emotional · External-validation
23, marketing intern. Checks prices on her commute, scrolls investment communities at night; needs social connection to ease anxiety.

Emily

Rational · Internal-belief
25, data analyst. Disciplined low-cost indexing; her main challenge is blocking out market noise.

Validate · How we run the experiments

Two studies, two questions

Study 1：the persona itself

Does the methodology produce a consistent and discriminable persona? Evaluated by expert designers, plus a perception scale.

Study 2：AI vs. human

How does the persona differ from real people? A researcher-led design probe interview, coded on six dimensions.

Study 1 validates the tool；Study 2 reveals the difference. Details and results on the next two slides.

Study 2 · Design & Result

A structurally excellent, authenticity-poor respondent

60-min design probe：Xiao-An + two screened participants (24 & 26). Three design concepts (past / present / future) as stimuli, coded on six dimensions (C1 to C6).

+43%
Structural (C1 reasoning + C5 coherence)：AI 5.0 / Human 3.5
−79%
Authenticity (C2 + C3 + C4 + C6)：Human 4.88 / AI 1.05

The AI is logically clear and complete, yet lacks the variability, friction, emotion, and thinking-delay of human talk.

Core Finding · Two modes of thinking

Synthetic　vs.　Experiential

AISynthetic

  • Pre-formed：the answer exists before the question ends
  • Immediacy · completeness · stability · predictability
  • Only 3 interaction markers · zero delay

HumanExperiential

  • Being-formed：thinking happens visibly while speaking
  • Temporality · fragmentation · emotion · unpredictability
  • 45 interaction markers · reflection ~4 min later
AI Personas as a Reference Baseline

Human "noise" is a trace of cognition

  • Hesitation：not unclear thought, but thinking-in-progress
  • Contradiction："I should hold long-term, but I keep checking" reveals rationality vs. emotion
  • Emotional disruption：an indicator of genuine involvement, not a defect

The AI's fluency makes these human signals visible. Its value isn't to simulate humans; it's to be a reference baseline.

Discussion · Complementary relationship

Different tools, not competitors

AI persona

Efficient, complete, always available, reproducible. Best for early exploration & hypothesis generation.

Human interview

Authentic friction, real emotion, unexpected insight. Best for later validation & depth.

AI-supported breadth + human-supported depth：a new standard configuration.

Toward a Living System

Consistency is the prerequisite for evolution

Static snapshot→Living system

Designer Interview Data

Transcripts, insights, user quotes feed the script, adding depth & context. Each interview becomes nourishment.

Automated CRM Data

Behavioral & transaction records update behavior and language, adding breadth & currency.

A consistent persona can be coherently updated, turning from a frozen document into a system that evolves. (Evolution mechanism = future work.)

Contributions

Three contributions

  • Methodological：a validated Six-Dimension Script Methodology for AI persona construction
  • Conceptual：synthetic vs. experiential thinking, understanding AI-human difference beyond fidelity
  • Practical：complementary positioning, AI for breadth, humans for depth
Limitations & Future Work

Scope and what's next

Limitations

  • 6 evaluators + 2 human interviewees
  • Single context (Gen Z ETF)
  • Specific AI model at a specific time

Future Work

  • Diverse user types and domains
  • Workflows for the living-system evolution
  • Co-design, prototype testing, team tools
Conclusion

From static snapshot
to a living system.

We built consistent, discriminable AI personas, revealed a structural difference from humans (synthetic vs. experiential thinking), and positioned AI as a complement that, once consistent, can evolve through continuous data. The difference is not a defect; it is what makes both the user and the researcher's practice visible.
