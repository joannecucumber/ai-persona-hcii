Background

Existing AI persona research asks "how human-like?" — we ask "how is it different from a real human?"

Static
📄
Personas: static documents.
Widely used in design research, but cannot capture how users dynamically change.
Interactive
💬
Generative AI: interactive agents.
But the difference from real participants is underexplored — we make it the data.
Research Questions

Can the persona stay coherent, and where does its language diverge from real participants?

RQ1How to build an AI persona that is consistent and discriminable?
RQ2What are the structural differences in language between AI personas and real participants?
Method · DSR

We treat the AI persona as a designed artifact and evaluate it through two studies.

Approach

We treat the AI persona as an artifact: construct, then evaluate across two studies (Hevner et al., 2004; Peffers et al., 2007).

Context：Gen Z ETF investors

Strong emotional-behavioral coupling："I should hold long-term, but I keep checking the market every hour."

Method · Construct

Four stages produce a persona that speaks in one consistent voice.

① Secondary

Gemini Deep Research × 2: round 1 finds two core axes + 4 user types; round 2 builds a full profile per quadrant.

② Primary

ChatGPT Deep Research: 30–50 first-person posts from PTT & Dcard as the persona's voice.

③ Script

Gemini integrates everything into a 7,500-word six-dimension script.

④ Deploy

Deployed as a GPT with 5 behavior constraints + role-internalization instructions.

GPT backend locks the script, so the role does not get overwritten in long conversations.

Method · Output

A six-dimension script holds two opposing characters stable across long conversations.

Findings · Two Modes

Real participants don't speak from a pre-formed self; they form thoughts in the act of speaking.

AI · Synthetic
"I was anxious too. My paper loss kept growing, I wanted to sell every day, checked the app three times a day."
0-sec delay · 3 interaction markers · answer exists before the question ends
Human · Experiential
"…seeing the money in the account keep dropping, I got pretty… panicked. wondering if it's a long-term trend, like…"
~4 min later before reflection surfaces · 45 interaction markers · thinking happens while speaking
Discussion

The AI's job is not to replace humans — it sets a structural baseline that makes real human signals visible.

  • Hesitation, contradiction, emotion in human talk are signals, not noise
  • The AI's fluency is what makes those signals stand out

AI persona

Efficient, always available. Best for early exploration.

Human interview

Authentic, unexpected. Best for later validation & depth.

Future · Living System

Get consistency first; then let the persona evolve as a living system fed by real-world data.

Conclusion

AI personas evolve from scripted snapshots into living systems that grow with continuous data.

AI personas do not replace humans, they complement. Once consistent, they can keep growing with continuous data.
