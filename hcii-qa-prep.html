A Core Argument · Difference-Analysis Positioning 3 questions

Why difference-analysis instead of the fidelity approach? Wouldn't a more human-like AI Persona be more useful?
為什麼是「差異分析取徑」而不是「擬真取徑」？越像真人的 AI Persona 不是更有用嗎？

The fidelity approach assumes humans are the standard AI should copy. But our numbers show something different — AI is 43% higher on structure, 79% lower on authenticity. That's not a defect. It's just a different kind of thinking.

So instead of asking "how close to human?", we ask "what does the gap tell us?" And it turns out the gap is where the useful signals are — the pauses, the contradictions, the emotion in a human interview.

擬真取徑預設「人是 AI 該模仿的標準」。但我們的資料顯示 AI 在結構性維度高 43%、真實性維度低 79%——這不是缺陷，是結構性差異。把差距當雜訊會抹掉訊號。

直接分析差異，可以把 AI Persona 定位成對照基準，讓真人訪談中的遲疑、矛盾、情緒流動被辨識為認知訊號而非雜訊。

openerThe fidelity approach quietly assumes the human is the target. We flip that — the difference itself is what's informative.
You call this "synthetic thinking" vs "experiential thinking." Isn't this just repackaging what we already know — that LLMs hallucinate?
你講「合成式思考」跟「經歷中的思考」——這不就是換個包裝在講 LLM 會幻覺嗎？

They're actually different things. Hallucination is about whether the answer is right or wrong. Synthetic thinking is about how the answer gets built — instantly, from patterns, all at once.

Human thinking is different. It unfolds over time, it's messy, it's emotional. So this isn't about fixing AI's mistakes. It's about seeing that AI thinks in a different way — and that changes how we should use it in research.

幻覺是輸出正確性的問題；合成式思考是認知過程的描述——兩者不同層次。幻覺說答案錯了，合成式思考描述的是答案如何從訓練模式即時組裝，沒有真人認知的時間性、片段性、情緒滲透。

這個區分把 AI 的「過度完整」從缺陷重框成不同的認知模式——這會改變我們該怎麼在研究中使用它。

Why do we need to interview an AI Persona at all — if we can just ask it for the answer directly?
為什麼要「訪談」AI Persona？如果它可以直接給答案，我直接問它不就好了？

Two reasons. First, interviewing gives you things designers actually need — the narrative, the tone, the little pauses. A direct Q&A doesn't give you that.

Second, the interview is what makes the AI Persona feel real to a design team. They can meet the character in dialogue and build intuitions together. So — direct Q&A gives you an answer. Interviewing gives you a character. That's still valuable, even if the character is AI.

兩個理由。第一，訪談會產出設計師需要的素材——敘事片段、語氣、猶豫模式，直接 Q&A 不會有。第二，訪談讓 AI Persona對設計團隊來說是可讀的角色，不只是研究者的工具。團隊可以透過對話認識角色、一起建立直覺。

直接 Q&A 給你答案；訪談給你一個角色，讓團隊可以圍繞它建立共識。這仍然有價值，即使角色是 AI。

riskThis is the sharpest question. If pushed, admit that direct-Q&A shortcuts many use cases, and reposition your contribution as "when the artifact of interviewing matters."

B Method Rigor · Sample, Coder, Fairness 3 questions

Only 2 human participants — how can you make a comparison claim from that?
只有 2 位真人受訪者，這樣可以做比較嗎？

This is a qualitative study, not a statistical one. Our unit isn't the person — it's the segment. Three design concepts, four questions each, six dimensions of coding. That gives us over 200 data points to look at structural patterns.

So the claim isn't "AI differs from all humans by 43%." It's "in this context, AI shows a consistent pattern of difference that's worth looking at more closely."

這是質性結構分析不是統計比較。分析單位不是「人」而是「segment」——三個設計方案 × 四題 = 12 segments / 人。加上六維度編碼，共 216 個資料點識別結構性模式。

我們主張的不是「AI 跟所有人差 43%」，而是「在可比較的情境下，AI 展現一致的不同認知輪廓，值得進一步探討」。

A single coder for the linguistic analysis — how do you defend the reliability?
語言分析只有你一個編碼者，信度怎麼確保？

Yeah, that's a real limitation. What we did to control it — every dimension has a concrete behavioral indicator. Like C2 counts filler words per 100 words. C6 measures the time between question and reflection. For edge cases we go back to earlier examples to stay consistent.

A second coder is the obvious next step. But honestly — the effects are so big, 43% and 79% — that a second coder would likely see the same pattern.

這是我們承認的限制。控制措施是：每個維度都有具體行為指標——例如 C2 算每百字語氣詞數、C6 測反思出現距離提問的時間差。邊界個案回看 anchor 校準避免漂移。

未來研究會加入第二位獨立編碼者。但目前效應量（43% 跟 -79%）夠大，第二編碼者應該會複製出方向，即使數字略有出入。

gambitAcknowledge honestly, then reframe: large effects don't disappear under a second coder.
Your humans spoke over voice; your AI Persona typed. That's an unfair comparison — the medium alone would explain the differences.
你的真人用語音、AI 用打字——這比較不公平，媒介本身就能解釋差異。

You're right — the medium is a confound. We should be more upfront about that. But two things — using emoji instead of emotion words, replying with zero delay, no self-correction — those aren't about voice or text. They're about how AI builds its answer.

And this setup — text AI, spoken humans — is exactly what a designer would face today. So the comparison is realistic. But yeah, testing AI Persona in voice mode is on our list for future work.

你說得對，媒介是 confound，這一點我們應該更清楚地說明。但兩個觀察：第一，我們幾個真實性指標——用 emoji 代替情緒詞、零延遲反思、缺乏 self-repair——反映的是 AI 的回應構成，不只是輸入方式。

第二，這正是設計師今天會遇到的實際情況——純文字 AI vs 語音訪談——所以這個比較反映生態效度。當然，未來把 AI Persona 放進語音模式測試是重要方向。

riskDon't deny it — concede the confound and reframe as ecological validity.

C Related Work Positioning 2 questions

How does your six-dimension script differ from Park et al.'s generative agents in 2023?
你的六維度劇本跟 Park 2023 的 Generative Agents 差在哪？

Three main differences. First, the goal — Park had 25 agents interacting in a sandbox to see what emerges. Ours is one persona for a designer to talk to in depth.

Second, what we provide — Park gives you an agent architecture, memory-reflection-planning. Ours gives you a way to build the persona itself. Park's seed is a short paragraph. Ours is a six-dimension framework.

Third, language — Park lets language emerge from the LLM. We treat language as its own dimension, and we source it from real community posts. That's a deliberate choice on our side.

三層差異。目的——Park 是模擬 25 個 agents 在沙盒的社群互動、產出湧現行為；我們建一個 persona 給設計師深度訪談。框架層次——Park 提供 agent 運行架構（memory-reflection-planning）；我們提供研究者建 persona 的方法論。Park 的 seed persona 只是一段文字；我們是系統化的六維度框架。

語言風格——Park 讓語言風格是 LLM 的湧現屬性；我們把它獨立成受控維度，主動從社群語料蒐集。這是研究者端的介入。

Recent work like PersonaCraft (Jung 2025) and Silicon Sampling (Argyle 2023) already generate personas at scale. What's new here?
最近 PersonaCraft (Jung 2025) 跟 Silicon Sampling (Argyle 2023) 都在做大規模 persona 生成，你的新意在哪？

Those works are about generating personas at scale, or simulating survey answers. They're evaluated on fidelity — how close to human data.

Our contribution is a different question. Instead of "how close?", we ask "where do they differ, and what does that mean for design research?" The new part is the reframe. AI Persona as a contrast, not a replacement. That's something a design team can actually work with — in a way "n=1000 synthetic users" isn't.

那些研究專注在大規模生成 persona 或用 LLM 模擬問卷回應——評估標準是擬真，也就是有多接近真人資料。我們的貢獻是正交的：不問「有多像真人」，而是問「結構上在哪裡分歧？這個差距對設計研究實務意義何在？」

新意在重新框架：AI Persona 是對照基準而非替代品——這對設計團隊是可行動的，而「n = 1000 synthetic users」不是。

D Synthetic vs Experiential Thinking 2 questions

You claim "no better or worse" — but structural clarity is clearly a strength. Isn't your framing avoiding the hard question?
你說「無優劣之分」——但結構清晰明明就是優勢，你這樣說是不是在迴避難的問題？

Structural clarity is a strength for some tasks — early exploration, hypothesis generation, concept testing. Authenticity is a strength for others — late-stage validation, unarticulated needs, building empathy. Neither wins across everything.

So "no better or worse" isn't neutrality. It's saying value depends on the task. Match the mode to the task — don't try to rank the modes.

結構清晰在某些設計任務是優勢——前期探索、假設生成、概念測試。真實性在其他任務是優勢——後期驗證、發掘難以言說的需求、建立同理。沒有哪一個能通吃所有任務。

「無優劣之分」不是中立立場——是價值取決於任務。我們是說：把模式對到任務，不要排名兩種模式。

Once LLMs improve, won't AI develop something like "experiential thinking" too? Your framework may age quickly.
LLM 未來會進步，AI 難道不會也發展出類似「經歷中的思考」？你的框架很快就會過時吧？

The framework describes today's architecture — not a permanent limit. If future models actually develop time-based, embodied, emotional cognition, we'd need to rethink.

But right now — a lot of "more human-like" AI efforts still simulate hesitation rather than experience it. Adding fake delays or filler words doesn't change how AI actually builds its answer — all at once, from patterns. So this difference may be architectural, not something you can train away.

這個框架描述的是當前架構的現實，不是永久限制。如果未來模型真的發展出時間性、具身、情緒滲透的認知，我們需要重新評估。

但注意：最近很多「更像人」的 LLM 努力還是在模擬猶豫而不是真的經歷。加人造延遲或語氣詞不改變底層的同步組裝式認知。這個區別可能更是架構層次的，不是訓練層次的。

gambitConcede on principle, then reassert: simulated hesitation ≠ experienced cognition.

E Application to Design Practice 2 questions

Has any actual design team adopted your "AI breadth + human depth" model? Or is this still speculative?
有真的設計團隊用過你的「AI 廣度 + 真人深度」模式嗎？還是這只是推論？

The model comes from our findings, and I don't have big field-deployment data yet. But the pattern is already showing up in practice — teams use AI to draft early user hypotheses, then validate with a smaller number of human interviews.

What we add is a reason for this split — based on how the two modes actually think — not just treating it as a resource trade-off.

這個互補模式是從我們的發現推導出來的，目前還沒有大規模的實地部署證據。但這個模式已經在實務中浮現——團隊用 AI 起草初步使用者假設，再用少量真人訪談驗證。

我們的貢獻是為這個分工提供理論根據（基於兩種思考模式）——而不是把它當作資源妥協。

If designers start with AI Personas, aren't they baking in bias that shapes what they hear from real users later?
如果設計師先用 AI Persona，會不會產生偏見、影響後面聽真實使用者的方式？

Yes, that's a real risk. That's why the critical stance matters. In our PPS results, credibility was actually the lowest score — 3.67 and 4.17 out of 7. That's a healthy signal — testers could tell what AI was getting wrong.

So we recommend treating AI Persona output as hypotheses to test, not conclusions. That's how "AI first, then human" is supposed to work. AI widens the search space. Humans define the answer.

對，這是真實的風險——所以批判性立場很重要。在我們的 PPS 結果裡，credibility 分數最低（3.67 / 4.17）。這是健康的訊號——測試者能辨識 AI 哪裡不對。

我們建議明確把 AI Persona 的產出當待驗證的假設，不是結論——這才是「AI 先、真人後」該有的運作方式。AI 的角色是擴大探索空間，不是定義答案。

F Limitations & Generalizability 2 questions

Your study is on Taiwanese Gen Z ETF investors. How does this generalize beyond that context?
你的研究只做台灣 Z 世代 ETF 投資者，怎麼推廣到其他情境？

The specific details are context-bound. Things like Anne using "haha" as punctuation — that's Taiwan Gen Z. But the structural patterns — zero-delay reflection, no self-repair, over-complete stories — those are architectural features of LLMs. They should generalize across languages and domains.

Applying the six-dimension methodology to other contexts — healthcare, legal, grief work — is explicit future work. That would test if the patterns hold.

具體發現——Anne 用「哈哈」當標點、社群語言重要——是情境特定的。但結構模式——AI 零延遲反思、缺 self-repair、過度完整敘事——是 LLM 的架構特徵，應該可以跨語言跨領域推廣。

把六維度方法論應用到其他情境（醫療、法律、悲傷輔導）是明確的未來研究方向——會測試這些模式是否成立。

LLMs move so fast — will your findings still be relevant in six months?
LLM 進步這麼快，你的發現半年後還有用嗎？

The specific numbers may shift as models improve. Our findings are a snapshot under specific conditions — we're upfront about that.

What should last is the methodology — using structural difference as a design-research lens. And the concept — synthetic vs experiential thinking. Even if AI closes the authenticity gap, the framework tells us what to look for to detect that shift.

具體的效應量可能會隨模型進步而變。但我們的發現是特定技術條件下的快照，我們也是這樣定位它的。

我們預期會持續的是方法論——用結構性差異作為設計研究的視角——以及概念框架（合成式 vs 經歷中的思考）。即使 AI 縮小了真實性差距，框架能告訴我們該觀察什麼來偵測這個變化。

G Curveballs · Unexpected Angles 1 question

If Anne is a "researcher-constructed artifact," how is she different from a stereotype? What makes her a persona and not a caricature?
如果 Anne 是「研究者建構的產物」，那她跟刻板印象差在哪？她憑什麼是 persona 而不是 caricature？

The difference is where the material comes from. A stereotype comes from cultural assumptions and researcher intuition. Anne is grounded in real material — Gen Z investor profiles plus 30 to 50 first-person posts from PTT and Dcard. Her language, her decisions, her emotions — you can trace them back to source material.

She's still researcher-mediated — I don't hide that in the paper. But the mediation is disciplined by real texts, not just by imagination. That's the same discipline that separates a good traditional persona from a caricature.

差異在素材來源。刻板印象來自文化預設跟研究者直覺；Anne 奠基於次級研究（Z 世代 ETF 投資者輪廓）+ 社群語料（30-50 則 PTT 跟 Dcard 第一人稱貼文）。她的語言、決策模式、情緒節奏都可追溯到原始素材。

她仍然是研究者中介的建構——我在論文裡不遮掩這件事。但中介被真實文本規訓，不是靠想像。這也是好的傳統 persona 跟 caricature 的差別。
