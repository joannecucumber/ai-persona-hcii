感謝這個問題，這其實是我們研究過程中反覆思考的部分。讓我從我們的做法談起。

🛟 Can't answer · 優雅轉場

"That's a really valuable perspective — we did discuss similar directions during the research. Given the time constraint here, I'd love to continue this conversation with you after the session, if that's okay."
這是一個很好的視角，在研究中我們也有類似的討論。因為時間的關係，如果可以，我希望用會後的時間跟你有更多的討論。

🎧 聽不清楚題目 · Can't Hear / Understand

國際會議 Q&A 讓對方重講是完全 OK 的——委員自己也常這樣做。不要硬答。四種分級策略，從輕到重使用。
① Blame the audio · 怪音訊（最好用）
ASorry, the audio broke up a little — could you repeat that? 抱歉音訊有點斷，可以再說一次嗎？
BI lost you for a second — could you say that again? 我剛剛沒聽到，可以再說一次嗎？
CSorry, could you speak a bit closer to the mic? 抱歉，可以離麥克風近一點嗎？
② Confirm · 確認你聽到什麼（同時買時間）
AJust to confirm — you're asking about [X]? 確認一下——你是在問 [X] 嗎？
BSo the question is about [X], right? 所以問題是關於 [X]，對嗎？
CIf I heard you correctly, you're asking [X] — is that right? 如果我聽對的話，你問的是 [X]，對嗎？
③ Rephrase · 請對方換句話說
AWould you mind rephrasing? I want to make sure I answer it well. 可以換個方式問嗎？我想確保我回答到位。
BCould you break it down into the key point? 可以拆解成核心問題嗎？
CCould you type the question in the chat as well? 可以把問題打在聊天室嗎？ ← 最強招
④ Pivot · 完全聽不懂就轉向你能談的
AI want to make sure I understand — are you asking about [topic A] or [topic B]? 我想確認——你是在問 [主題 A] 還是 [主題 B]？（給兩個你能答的選項）
BLet me answer what I think is the core of your question — [start talking about what you can]. 讓我針對我理解到的核心來回答——（開始講你能講的）
CThat's a great question — I want to give it the attention it deserves. Could we discuss this after the session? 這是很好的問題，我想給它應有的深度。可以會後討論嗎？（核彈選項，優雅退場）

🎯 Universal patterns · 萬用回應

四種通用句型，任何題目都可以套。找不到對應答案時，先靠句型撐住場面。
① Topic Opener · 主題開場（取代 Yes/No）
AOn [topic], the way we think about it is... 關於 [主題]，我們的看法是...
BThere are two parts to this. First... Second... 這裡有兩個部分。第一... 第二...
CWhat we found in the data is... 我們在資料中發現的是...
DThe short answer is [X]. The longer answer is... 簡短回答是 [X]。展開講的話...
② Partial Agreement + Pivot · 承認+轉場（尖銳問題）
AThat's a fair concern. Where we land is... 這是合理的顧慮。我們的立場是...
BThere's truth to that. What we add is... 確實有這個部分。我們補上的是...
CI'd frame it a bit differently. Instead of X, we're doing Y. 我會換個角度看。我們做的不是 X，是 Y。
DYou're touching on something we thought a lot about. Our position is... 這個點我們反覆思考過。我們的立場是...
③ Honest "Not Sure" · 誠實不確定（不掉價）
AI don't have a definitive answer. My best interpretation would be... 我沒有確定的答案。我目前的看法是...
BWe haven't tested that directly. Based on what we did find... 這個我們沒有直接測。但根據我們的發現...
CThat's beyond our scope, but the closest signal we have is... 這超出我們的研究範圍，但最接近的訊號是...
DThat's honestly something we're still working through. 老實說這是我們還在思考的部分。
④ Wrap-up · 收尾（把球丟回去）
ADoes that address your question? 這樣有回答到你的問題嗎？
BHappy to go deeper if useful. 如果有幫助，可以再深入談。
CI hope that gives you a sense of where we're coming from. 希望這讓你感受到我們的出發點。

A Core Argument · Difference-Analysis Positioning 核心論點 · 差異分析取徑 3 questions

Why difference-analysis instead of the fidelity approach? Wouldn't a more human-like AI Persona be more useful?
為什麼是「差異分析取徑」而不是「擬真取徑」？越像真人的 AI Persona 不是更有用嗎？

Most research asks — how close is AI to a real person? We ask something different. What does the gap tell us?

In our data, AI is much better at structure. But 79% weaker at things like pauses and emotion. That gap is actually useful. It shows what a real human interview gives you.

擬真取徑預設「人是 AI 該模仿的標準」。但我們的資料顯示 AI 在結構性維度高 43%、真實性維度低 79%——這不是缺陷，是結構性差異。把差距當雜訊會抹掉訊號。

直接分析差異，可以把 AI Persona 定位成對照基準，讓真人訪談中的遲疑、矛盾、情緒流動被辨識為認知訊號而非雜訊。

openerThe fidelity approach quietly assumes the human is the target. We flip that — the difference itself is what's informative.
You call this "synthetic thinking" vs "experiential thinking." Isn't this just repackaging what we already know — that LLMs hallucinate?
你講「合成式思考」跟「經歷中的思考」——這不就是換個包裝在講 LLM 會幻覺嗎？

They are different. Hallucination is about answers being wrong. Synthetic thinking is about how the answer gets built — all at once, from patterns.

Human thinking is not like that. It takes time. It's messy. It has emotion. So we're not fixing AI's mistakes. We're saying AI thinks in a different way. And that changes how we should use it.

幻覺是輸出正確性的問題；合成式思考是認知過程的描述——兩者不同層次。幻覺說答案錯了，合成式思考描述的是答案如何從訓練模式即時組裝，沒有真人認知的時間性、片段性、情緒滲透。

這個區分把 AI 的「過度完整」從缺陷重框成不同的認知模式——這會改變我們該怎麼在研究中使用它。

Why do we need to interview an AI Persona at all — if we can just ask it for the answer directly?
為什麼要「訪談」AI Persona？如果它可以直接給答案，我直接問它不就好了？

Two reasons. First, an interview gives you things a Q&A doesn't. The tone, the story, the small pauses. Designers need these things.

Second, an interview makes the AI Persona feel real to the team. They can meet the character, not just read a data sheet. So — a Q&A gives you an answer. An interview gives you a character. That's valuable, even for AI.

兩個理由。第一，訪談會產出設計師需要的素材——敘事片段、語氣、猶豫模式，直接 Q&A 不會有。第二，訪談讓 AI Persona對設計團隊來說是可讀的角色，不只是研究者的工具。團隊可以透過對話認識角色、一起建立直覺。

直接 Q&A 給你答案；訪談給你一個角色，讓團隊可以圍繞它建立共識。這仍然有價值，即使角色是 AI。

riskThis is the sharpest question. If pushed, admit that direct-Q&A shortcuts many use cases, and reposition your contribution as "when the artifact of interviewing matters."

B Method Rigor · Sample, Coder, Fairness 方法嚴謹性 · 樣本、編碼、比較公平性 3 questions

Only 2 human participants — how can you make a comparison claim from that?
只有 2 位真人受訪者，這樣可以做比較嗎？

It's a qualitative study, not a statistical one. What we count is not the person. It's each answer segment. Three concepts, four questions each, six dimensions of coding. So we have over 200 data points.

We are not saying "AI is 43% different from all humans." We are saying — in this setting, AI shows a clear pattern of difference. And that pattern is worth exploring.

這是質性結構分析不是統計比較。分析單位不是「人」而是「segment」——三個設計方案 × 四題 = 12 segments / 人。加上六維度編碼，共 216 個資料點識別結構性模式。

我們主張的不是「AI 跟所有人差 43%」，而是「在可比較的情境下，AI 展現一致的不同認知輪廓，值得進一步探討」。

A single coder for the linguistic analysis — how do you defend the reliability?
語言分析只有你一個編碼者，信度怎麼確保？

On coding reliability — every dimension has a clear rule. Like — C2 counts filler words per 100 words. C6 measures the time between question and reflection. For edge cases we compare back to earlier examples.

A second coder would be the next step. But the effects are so big — 43% and 79% — a second coder would likely see the same pattern.

編碼信度上——每個維度都有明確的規則。例如 C2 算每百字語氣詞數、C6 測反思出現距離提問的時間差。邊界個案回看 anchor 校準避免漂移。

加入第二位獨立編碼者會是下一步。但目前效應量（43% 跟 -79%）夠大，第二編碼者應該會看到相同的模式。

gambitAcknowledge honestly, then reframe: large effects don't disappear under a second coder.
Your humans spoke over voice; your AI Persona typed. That's an unfair comparison — the medium alone would explain the differences.
你的真人用語音、AI 用打字——這比較不公平，媒介本身就能解釋差異。

On the medium — voice versus text does matter. But some of what we see — using emoji for emotion, replying instantly, never correcting itself — these are not about voice or text. They are about how AI builds an answer.

And this setup — text AI, spoken humans — is what a designer would meet today. So the comparison is realistic. Testing AI Persona in voice mode is on our list for future work.

關於媒介——語音跟打字的差異確實存在。但我們看到的一些現象——用 emoji 代替情緒詞、零延遲反思、缺乏 self-repair——這些反映的是 AI 建構回應的方式，不只是輸入模態的差異。

另外，這個設定——純文字 AI vs 語音真人——正是設計師今天會遇到的實際情況，所以這個比較有實務意義。把 AI Persona 放進語音模式測試是未來研究的方向。

riskDon't deny it — concede the confound and reframe as ecological validity.

C Related Work Positioning 相關研究定位 2 questions

How does your six-dimension script differ from Park et al.'s generative agents in 2023?
你的六維度劇本跟 Park 2023 的 Generative Agents 差在哪？

Three main differences. First, the goal. Park had 25 agents talking in a sandbox to see what happens. Ours is one persona for a designer to interview in depth.

Second, what each work gives you. Park gives an agent architecture. Ours gives a way to build the persona itself. Park's starting point is a short paragraph. Ours is a six-dimension framework.

Third, language. Park lets language emerge from the LLM. We treat language as its own dimension. We source it from real community posts. It's a deliberate choice.

三層差異。目的——Park 是模擬 25 個 agents 在沙盒的社群互動、產出湧現行為；我們建一個 persona 給設計師深度訪談。框架層次——Park 提供 agent 運行架構（memory-reflection-planning）；我們提供研究者建 persona 的方法論。Park 的 seed persona 只是一段文字；我們是系統化的六維度框架。

語言風格——Park 讓語言風格是 LLM 的湧現屬性；我們把它獨立成受控維度，主動從社群語料蒐集。這是研究者端的介入。

Recent work like PersonaCraft (Jung 2025) and Silicon Sampling (Argyle 2023) already generate personas at scale. What's new here?
最近 PersonaCraft (Jung 2025) 跟 Silicon Sampling (Argyle 2023) 都在做大規模 persona 生成，你的新意在哪？

Those works generate personas at scale, or simulate survey answers. They ask — how close to human? Our question is different. We ask — where is the difference? And what does it mean for design research?

So we don't see AI Persona as a replacement. We see it as a contrast. That's something a design team can actually work with. "n=1000 synthetic users" — is not.

那些研究專注在大規模生成 persona 或用 LLM 模擬問卷回應——評估標準是擬真，也就是有多接近真人資料。我們的貢獻是正交的：不問「有多像真人」，而是問「結構上在哪裡分歧？這個差距對設計研究實務意義何在？」

新意在重新框架：AI Persona 是對照基準而非替代品——這對設計團隊是可行動的，而「n = 1000 synthetic users」不是。

D Synthetic vs Experiential Thinking 合成式 vs 經歷中的思考 2 questions

You claim "no better or worse" — but structural clarity is clearly a strength. Isn't your framing avoiding the hard question?
你說「無優劣之分」——但結構清晰明明就是優勢，你這樣說是不是在迴避難的問題？

Structural clarity is good for some tasks. Like early exploration, brainstorming, quick tests. Authenticity is good for other tasks. Like deep validation, finding hidden needs, building empathy. Neither one wins for everything.

So "no better or worse" isn't neutral. It means — value depends on the task. Match the mode to the task. Don't try to rank the modes.

結構清晰在某些設計任務是優勢——前期探索、假設生成、概念測試。真實性在其他任務是優勢——後期驗證、發掘難以言說的需求、建立同理。沒有哪一個能通吃所有任務。

「無優劣之分」不是中立立場——是價值取決於任務。我們是說：把模式對到任務，不要排名兩種模式。

Once LLMs improve, won't AI develop something like "experiential thinking" too? Your framework may age quickly.
LLM 未來會進步，AI 難道不會也發展出類似「經歷中的思考」？你的框架很快就會過時吧？

The framework describes today's architecture. Not a permanent limit. If future AI really develops time-based, embodied, emotional thinking, yes — we would need to rethink.

But right now — many "more human-like" AI efforts just simulate hesitation. Adding fake delays or filler words doesn't change how AI builds its answer. It still happens all at once. So this may be architectural — not something you can train away.

這個框架描述的是當前架構的現實，不是永久限制。如果未來模型真的發展出時間性、具身、情緒滲透的認知，我們需要重新評估。

但注意：最近很多「更像人」的 LLM 努力還是在模擬猶豫而不是真的經歷。加人造延遲或語氣詞不改變底層的同步組裝式認知。這個區別可能更是架構層次的，不是訓練層次的。

gambitConcede on principle, then reassert: simulated hesitation ≠ experienced cognition.

E Application to Design Practice 對設計實務的應用 2 questions

Has any actual design team adopted your "AI breadth + human depth" model? Or is this still speculative?
有真的設計團隊用過你的「AI 廣度 + 真人深度」模式嗎？還是這只是推論？

The model comes from our findings. I don't have big deployment data yet. But this pattern is already showing up. Teams use AI to draft early hypotheses. Then they validate with a smaller number of human interviews.

What we add is a reason for this split. Based on how the two modes think. Not just a resource trade-off.

這個互補模式是從我們的發現推導出來的，目前還沒有大規模的實地部署證據。但這個模式已經在實務中浮現——團隊用 AI 起草初步使用者假設，再用少量真人訪談驗證。

我們的貢獻是為這個分工提供理論根據（基於兩種思考模式）——而不是把它當作資源妥協。

If designers start with AI Personas, aren't they baking in bias that shapes what they hear from real users later?
如果設計師先用 AI Persona，會不會產生偏見、影響後面聽真實使用者的方式？

The way to handle bias is to stay critical of the AI output. In our PPS results, credibility was actually the lowest — 3.67 and 4.17 out of 7. That's a healthy sign. Testers could tell what AI was getting wrong.

So we suggest treating AI output as a hypothesis to test. Not a conclusion. That's how "AI first, then human" should work. AI opens the search. Humans define the answer.

處理偏見的方式是對 AI 產出保持批判。在我們的 PPS 結果裡，credibility 分數最低（3.67 / 4.17）。這是健康的訊號——測試者能辨識 AI 哪裡不對。

我們建議明確把 AI Persona 的產出當待驗證的假設，不是結論——這才是「AI 先、真人後」該有的運作方式。AI 的角色是擴大探索空間，不是定義答案。

F Limitations & Generalizability 研究限制與推廣性 2 questions

Your study is on Taiwanese Gen Z ETF investors. How does this generalize beyond that context?
你的研究只做台灣 Z 世代 ETF 投資者，怎麼推廣到其他情境？

The specific details are context-bound. Like Anne using "haha" as punctuation — that's Taiwan Gen Z. But the structural patterns — no delay, no self-correction, over-complete stories — these come from how LLMs work. They should hold across languages and domains.

Applying the six-dimension method to other contexts — healthcare, legal, grief work — is future work. That would test if the patterns hold.

具體發現——Anne 用「哈哈」當標點、社群語言重要——是情境特定的。但結構模式——AI 零延遲反思、缺 self-repair、過度完整敘事——是 LLM 的架構特徵，應該可以跨語言跨領域推廣。

把六維度方法論應用到其他情境（醫療、法律、悲傷輔導）是明確的未來研究方向——會測試這些模式是否成立。

LLMs move so fast — will your findings still be relevant in six months?
LLM 進步這麼快，你的發現半年後還有用嗎？

The specific numbers may change as models improve. Our findings are a snapshot under today's conditions. We're clear about that.

What should last is the method — using structural difference to look at design research. And the concept — synthetic vs experiential thinking. Even if AI closes the gap, the framework tells us what to look for.

具體的效應量可能會隨模型進步而變。但我們的發現是特定技術條件下的快照，我們也是這樣定位它的。

我們預期會持續的是方法論——用結構性差異作為設計研究的視角——以及概念框架（合成式 vs 經歷中的思考）。即使 AI 縮小了真實性差距，框架能告訴我們該觀察什麼來偵測這個變化。

G Curveballs · Unexpected Angles 尖銳題 · 意外角度 1 question

If Anne is a "researcher-constructed artifact," how is she different from a stereotype? What makes her a persona and not a caricature?
如果 Anne 是「研究者建構的產物」，那她跟刻板印象差在哪？她憑什麼是 persona 而不是 caricature？

The difference is where the material comes from. A stereotype comes from cultural guesses and researcher intuition. Anne is built from real material — Gen Z investor profiles, plus 30 to 50 first-person posts from PTT and Dcard. Her words, her decisions, her emotions — you can trace them back to sources.

She is still shaped by the researcher — I don't hide that. But she's shaped by real texts, not just imagination. That's the same discipline that separates a good traditional persona from a caricature.

差異在素材來源。刻板印象來自文化預設跟研究者直覺；Anne 奠基於次級研究（Z 世代 ETF 投資者輪廓）+ 社群語料（30-50 則 PTT 跟 Dcard 第一人稱貼文）。她的語言、決策模式、情緒節奏都可追溯到原始素材。

她仍然是研究者中介的建構——我在論文裡不遮掩這件事。但中介被真實文本規訓，不是靠想像。這也是好的傳統 persona 跟 caricature 的差別。

gambitOwn the construction, then defend the discipline.
